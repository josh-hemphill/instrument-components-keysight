Connectors enforce the finite deadline and256-byte allocation before returning a complete plain-text body. Blocking host access cannot be made deadline-safe by abandoning a live host thread. Native hosts must terminate active accesses before cancellation completes. The SDK owns whole-operation serialization and partial-acquisition/worker finalization; device engines do not duplicate those algorithms. Close releases resources and never promises electrical shutdown.
